01

About GDGOC

Google Developer Groups on Campus (GDGOC) are student-led communities for university students who want to explore technology. No gatekeeping: you don't need to be an expert, you just need to be curious.

Our chapter at COMSATS Attock exists to give students a place to practise what they study, meet people who build things, and get a head start on the skills the industry keeps asking for.

  • Learn

    Workshops, study jams and talks that go past the syllabus.

  • Collaborate

    Team up across departments on real projects instead of solo assignments.

  • Grow

    Lead a team, speak on a stage, build a portfolio, find your people.

04

GDGOC Teams

Seven teams, one community. Pick the one that sounds like you (you can change your mind later).

Web Team

Builds and maintains the chapter's websites and web tools, and runs HTML, CSS and JavaScript sessions.

App Team

Mobile and cross-platform development: Android, Flutter and everything that lives in your pocket.

Media Team

Photography, video, graphics and design. If it looks good on the feed, this team made it.

PR Team

Handles outreach, partnerships and communication with students, faculty and other communities.

AI/ML Team

Explores machine learning and AI through study groups, small projects and hands-on experiments.

Management Team

Keeps everything running: planning events, coordinating teams, logistics and deadlines.

Women in Tech Team

Creates a supportive space for women in technology through mentorship, sessions and networking.
